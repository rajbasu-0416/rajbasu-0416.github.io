01. About

I'm an AI Engineer at TeamLogic IT, where I design and ship GenAI applications and agent-based workflows for internal business teams. Before that I spent four and a half years at Wipro, first automating enterprise processes as an RPA developer, then building GenAI proofs of concept for clients as an AI Engineer.

My work sits where LLMs meet real business data. I like turning an ambiguous problem ("we need an answer sooner") into a governed product: a clear spec, a narrow and testable role for the model, deterministic code for anything that must be exact, and the logging, backtests and feedback loops that show whether it's actually working.

I studied Electrical Engineering at JIS College of Engineering, and moved into software through automation, which still shapes how I think about AI: it should remove manual effort reliably, not just demo well.

03. Featured work

2026 · Owned end to end · In production

AI-assisted project effort estimation

problem

Business teams needed a fast, evidence-based effort estimate early in the sales cycle, without waiting for a detailed estimate from delivery teams.

solution

A web tool that finds similar past projects, uses an LLM to judge relevance and interpret the scope description, then calculates the estimate and range in code, so the model never produces the numbers.

production readiness

Guardrails, access control, monitoring and alerting, an evaluation harness to measure accuracy, and a user feedback loop for continuous tuning.
